KidsJellyFin Privacy Policy
Effective October 4, 2026

KidsJellyFin connects to a Jellyfin server you choose. The developer does not host your media or operate an analytics service for this app.

The app sends authentication, catalog, artwork, streaming, and playback-report requests to your server. Its operator can see your account, app/device information, network address, requests, and viewing activity, and controls server logging and retention. Use a trusted server.

On Apple TV, access tokens use Apple's Keychain. Connection and parent setup, approved library identifiers, playback positions, completion, shuffle choices, and preferences are stored locally. Artwork and technical diagnostics may be cached locally. Opt-in performance diagnostics exclude credentials, media titles, server URLs, and RAID paths and are not automatically sent to the developer.

When provisioned and available, private Apple CloudKit sync can store playback progress, shuffle choices, viewing-session state, preferences, and account/library identifiers. It excludes passwords, tokens, the parent PIN, media, artwork, and catalog metadata. Apple's privacy terms govern iCloud.

There are no ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. The app does not ask children for personal or contact information. Parents configure access and select suitable content.

Parents can reset playback state and remove saved accounts. App deletion, Keychain, server records, iCloud records, and backups have separate retention; a progress reset does not promise erasure of all copies. Use server and Apple controls for those records.

For the full policy and support contact, see the links in this App Store listing. Do not post credentials, private media information, or children's data in public support requests.
