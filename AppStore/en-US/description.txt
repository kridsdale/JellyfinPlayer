Family TV, made simple.

KidsJellyFin brings the shows and movies you have chosen for your family to Apple TV, with a picture-led interface designed for children who are still learning to read.

TWO PLACES TO EXPLORE
Browse Shows and Movies through large artwork. KidsJellyFin uses only the Kid TV and Kid Movies libraries on your Jellyfin server.

NEXT OR SHUFFLE
Choose a show, then press Next to continue in order or Shuffle for a surprise. Shuffle keeps track of its choices to reduce repeats within a cycle. Picking an individual season or episode lives in the protected Parents area.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Resume an unfinished episode or movie. Pause, seek, and return to browsing with the Apple TV remote.

PARENTS HANDLE THE DETAILS
A parent PIN protects setup, episode selection, playback preferences, and connection help. Use your Jellyfin server to maintain the approved libraries and a restricted account to control access. Parents remain responsible for choosing suitable content.

BRING YOUR OWN SERVER AND MEDIA
KidsJellyFin requires a compatible Jellyfin server, network access to it, and a non-administrator Jellyfin account with access limited to libraries named Kid TV and Kid Movies. Setup is required before children can watch. The app does not include movies, television shows, or a streaming subscription.

KidsJellyFin is an independent client derived from the open-source Swiftfin project. Jellyfin and Swiftfin are separate open-source projects.
