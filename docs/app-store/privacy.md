KidsJellyFin Privacy Policy
Effective October 4, 2026

KidsJellyFin is an independent Apple TV client for a Jellyfin server you choose. The developer does not operate a media hosting service or an analytics service for this app.

Your Jellyfin server
The app sends authentication requests, library and artwork requests, streaming requests, and playback reports to your configured Jellyfin server. These requests identify your Jellyfin account, the app/device, and the media being played. The server can observe your network address, requests, and playback activity. Its operator controls access, logging, and retention. Do not use an untrusted server. The app does not upload your media files to the developer.

On the Apple TV
Account access tokens are stored using Apple's Keychain. The app stores its server connection, approved library identifiers, parent setup, playback positions, completion state, shuffle choices, and playback preferences in local app storage. Temporary artwork and technical diagnostics may be stored locally. Performance profiling is opt-in and is designed to exclude credentials, media titles, server URLs, and RAID paths. These diagnostics are not automatically sent to the developer.

iCloud
When iCloud synchronization is provisioned and available, Apple CloudKit may synchronize playback positions, completion state, shuffle choices, viewing-session state, playback preferences, and the identifiers needed to keep different Jellyfin accounts and libraries separate. Records use your private iCloud database. This progress store does not synchronize Jellyfin passwords or access tokens, the parent PIN, media files, artwork, or the media catalog. Apple's terms and privacy policy govern iCloud. Local playback state can also work without an iCloud connection.

Advertising and children
The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. It does not ask children for names, email addresses, location, or contact information. Parents configure the connection and select suitable media on their own server. The parent PIN is a local navigation control; parents should also restrict the Jellyfin account and Apple TV settings.

Control and deletion
Parents can reset playback progress and preferences from the Parents area. Removing a saved account removes its local connection credentials through the app's account controls. App deletion removes local app data, but Keychain items, your Jellyfin server records, and previously synchronized iCloud records can have separate retention. Contact the server operator or use Apple's iCloud controls for those records. A progress reset synchronizes as a reset; it does not promise erasure of Apple's backups or server logs.

Questions
Contact the maintainer through the support page linked in the App Store listing. Do not post passwords, access tokens, server addresses, private media titles, or children's personal information in public support requests.
